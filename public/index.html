    How a round is judged

    1. 1

      Judges check in at the venue

      Scoring stays locked until the judge's location is inside the venue radius.

    2. 2

      Each judge scores the live round

      Autonomy, design and documentation, out of 10. Drafts save as you go.

    3. 3

      Teams get their averages

      Results are averaged across the panel. No team sees which judge gave what.
